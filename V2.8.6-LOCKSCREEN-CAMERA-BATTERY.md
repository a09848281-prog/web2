# Remote Camera Web2 v2.8.6 — Lock-screen camera recovery & battery forecast

- Base: v2.8.5 (`preview/v2.8.5-reconnect-watchdog`)
- Branch: `preview/v2.8.6-lockscreen-camera-battery`
- Android: `2.8.6-preview`, versionCode `435`, applicationId `jp.a09848281.remotecamera.v31`
- GAS API: unchanged (`1.4.0`); no GAS redeployment required.

## Lock screen / biometric authentication
- Camera capturer receives Camera2 error, disconnect and freeze callbacks.
- On temporary camera contention, only the camera capturer is retried. Existing PeerConnections, video source/track, audio track and DataChannels remain in place.
- Web receives a camera-recovery flag and temporarily preserves RTC control while Android reacquires the camera.
- Activity resume/pause is UI-only and does not start/stop the foreground service or RTC.
- Android may still deny camera access while system authentication owns it; PIN/password/biometrics are not bypassed.

## Battery forecast
- Web selector allows 0–100% in 5% steps, independent of auto-disconnect. Changing it does not disconnect monitoring.
- Fuel-gauge charge counter + averaged current are sampled and smoothed using the median of recent readings where supported.
- Otherwise a rolling least-squares discharge-rate estimate is used.
- If no usable estimate exists after 30 minutes, Web reports `推定データ不足` instead of endless calculating.
- Runtime remains an estimate affected by temperature, screen, network, camera and workload.

## Compatibility
- New package suffix `.v31`; v2.8.5 `.v30` remains intact. Install and pair this APK separately.
- GAS deployment unchanged.
